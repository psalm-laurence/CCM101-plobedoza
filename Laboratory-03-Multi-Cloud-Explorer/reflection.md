# Reflection

In this laboratory activity, I learned more about AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Among the three cloud platforms, AWS impressed me the most because it offers many different services for computing, storage, networking, databases, and security. I found it useful because it can support different types of businesses, from small startups to large companies. However, I also learned that Azure and GCP can be better choices depending on the needs of a specific organization.

While comparing the three platforms, I noticed that AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. They all provide virtual machines, cloud storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also allow users to access computing resources through the internet without having to manage all the physical hardware. Although their services are similar, each platform has different features and strengths.

I learned that business requirements have a big influence when choosing a cloud platform. Companies need to consider their budget, existing technology, security needs, number of users, and future growth. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products can benefit from Azure, while an AI research company may choose GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning services. A startup or global e-commerce company may choose AWS because of its scalability and wide range of services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that selecting a cloud provider requires understanding the client's actual needs. It is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. I learned how to compare services and recommend a suitable provider based on different business situations.

Lastly, creating my GitHub portfolio improved my learning because it helped me organize my research, documentation, and activities in one place. It also allowed me to practice Markdown, GitHub, and proper documentation. Overall, this activity improved my understanding of cloud platforms and helped me develop skills that can be useful in future IT and cloud-related work.
